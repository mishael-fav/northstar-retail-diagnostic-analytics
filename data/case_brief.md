# Northstar Retail — Diagnostic Analytics Case Study

## Business context
Northstar Retail is a multi-region e-commerce business operating across Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.

Management has noticed that Q2 2025 net revenue appears materially weaker than Q2 2024 even though site traffic and marketing activity do not appear to have fallen.

## Stakeholder request
The VP of Growth has asked you to determine:
- What is happening?
- When did the problem begin?
- Which parts of the business are driving it?
- What should the team investigate next?

## Competing internal explanations
- Marketing: "Traffic quality has deteriorated."
- Product: "This is probably seasonality."
- Operations: "Returns are the main problem."
- Finance: "Marketing spend is rising faster than revenue."

Do not assume any of these explanations is correct.

## Your assignment
Use the V → T → D → B → C framework:

**Validate → Trend → Decompose → Benchmark → Conclude**

1. Validate the datasets before analysis.
2. Define the key business metrics.
3. Determine whether Q2 2025 is genuinely abnormal.
4. Identify when the performance shift began.
5. Decompose the change by device, channel, region, category, and funnel stage.
6. Determine whether the problem is primarily traffic, conversion, average order value, discounts, refunds, or a combination.
7. Evaluate marketing efficiency without double-counting spend.
8. Separate facts, interpretations, and hypotheses.
9. Produce 2–6 decision-useful visualizations.
10. Write a concise executive summary with 3–6 important findings and recommended next actions.

## Suggested metrics
- Net Revenue = Gross Revenue − Discount Amount − Refund Amount
- Conversion Rate = Orders ÷ Sessions
- Add-to-Cart Rate = Add_to_Cart ÷ Sessions
- Checkout Start Rate = Checkout_Starts ÷ Add_to_Cart
- Checkout Completion Rate = Orders ÷ Checkout_Starts
- Average Order Value = Gross Revenue ÷ Orders
- ROAS = Net Revenue from paid channels ÷ Marketing Spend

## Deliverables
A. Analysis notes / question tree  
B. Clean calculations  
C. Visualizations  
D. Written stakeholder analysis  
E. A section called "What the data cannot prove"

## Important
The release log provides context only. A release occurring near a metric change does not prove causation.
